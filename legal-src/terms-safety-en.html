Reporting and blocking

There is no tolerance on Nidi for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. This covers everything shared in a nidi: voice notes, photos, words and notes.

Nidi does not review moments before they are shared, but anyone in a nidi can report a moment or block the person who shared it: tap the three dots (⋯) on that moment and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides what that person's home has shared, keeps anything new from them from reaching you, and tells us. Nothing is deleted, and you can unblock at any time.

We review every report and every block within 24 hours. You can also write to hello@nidi.life.
