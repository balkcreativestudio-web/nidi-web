Less screen, more together

Let children be children.

We don't always have time for a call, and a baby or a toddler won't sit through one anyway. So Nidi gives you ideas for small things to do together, each in your own home, and a way to share them afterwards.

Why Nidi exists

Why I made Nidi.

I'm an Argentine mother in the Netherlands. When my daughter was born, my parents were 11,000 km away, and video calls with a baby don't work. I wanted her to grow up knowing her grandparents' voices, their stories, the small things they do every day. Not just faces on a screen. So I built Nidi.

Nidi connects two houses: the child's, and one far away. Every day it suggests small things to do, apart but together. A grandmother records a good morning in her voice. Both houses photograph the same sky, or cook the same recipe. The screen carries it; the moment happens off it. It all lands in Memory, an archive that stays with the family.

Since we started testing, my parents have read my daughter bedtime stories, sung to her at breakfast, and left a good morning every day, recorded the night before in Buenos Aires.

My daughter is not yet two. She knows her grandparents' voices.

Questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Is Nidi free?
Two free weeks, with no payment details. Then the person who started the nidi chooses a monthly or yearly subscription.
Who pays?
Only the person who starts a nidi, and one subscription covers up to three nidis. The people they invite never pay.
Does the other home need the app?
Yes. They install Nidi, sign in with a code sent to their email, and join with your invitation. It is free for them.
Who can see what we share?
Only the people in your nidi. Nothing is public or searchable.
What languages does it speak?
English and Spanish, each person in their own language. Voice notes in either can arrive written out.
How old is the child?
From the first months to six years old, with activities chosen for their age. Up to three, the home where the child lives also keeps their firsts.
What happens if we stop the subscription?
The nidi pauses for now and nothing new can be added, but everything already shared stays in Memory for anyone in it. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your iPhone's Settings.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. Nidi comes first to iPhone, and Android is on its way. Leave your email and we will write when it is ready.
